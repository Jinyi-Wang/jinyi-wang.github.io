👋 About Me

Hello! I am Jinyi Wang (Jin-yi, pronounced “Gin-e”).

I am currently a PhD student at Linköping University (2023–present).

I received my Master's degree in Computer Science from Beijing University of Technology.


In my daily life, I often find myself asking "why": Why does this happen? What leads people to act the way they do? What process produced this outcome?

I am fascinated by the gap between what we observe and the underlying mechanisms that produce it.

Whether it is a user behavior, a reasoning process, or everyday phenomena, I enjoy exploring the hidden mechanisms that produce observable outcomes.

📊 Research Interests

Research keywords:

  • Visual Analytics
  • User Intention and Decision Making
  • Sensemaking and Reasoning Processes

I am interested in developing methods and tools that help researchers and practitioners understand users' intentions, reasoning process, and decision-making behaviors when interacting with complex systems.

My work focuses on moving from interaction traces and observable behaviors towards explanations of underlying intentions and reasoning processes.
